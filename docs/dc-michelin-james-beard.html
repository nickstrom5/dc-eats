DC MICHELIN & James Beard restaurants

118 District restaurants recognized by MICHELIN or the James Beard Foundation. The MICHELIN Guide Washington, D.C. lists 107 places here (two 2-Star, 18 1-Star, 28 Bib Gourmand and 59 Recommended restaurants); 16 places hold a James Beard award, a 2026 nomination or semifinalist nod, or an America's Classics award.

What counts as an honor here

Two sources, both reported as facts rather than scores. One is the MICHELIN Guide Washington, D.C., as its pages read in fall 2026, including its Green Star. The other is the James Beard Foundation: its winners, its 2026 nominees and semifinalists, and America's Classics awards from any year (2 places). Semifinalists are labeled as exactly that, and where an honor went to a chef, the line under the place says so. Reader polls, "best of" roundups and review-site stars don't count.

MICHELIN and the MICHELIN Guide are trademarks of Michelin. James Beard Foundation and James Beard Award are trademarks of the James Beard Foundation. DC Eats is not affiliated with either.
